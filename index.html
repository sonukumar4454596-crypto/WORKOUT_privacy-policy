2. Data Collection and Usage

To provide personalized workout plans, rest timers, and exercise tracking, the App stores and calculates certain data parameters directly on your device:

A. Fitness & Profile Information (Voluntarily Provided)

  • Physical Parameters: Gender, age range, height, weight, and fitness goal (e.g., muscle gain, fat loss, stamina).
  • Workout Preferences: Target muscle focus areas (Chest, Arms, Abs, Legs, Full Body), workout style (Hypertrophy, Strength, HIIT), experience level, and available equipment.
  • Where it is stored: Stored locally in Android App Sandboxed Storage (LocalStorage / SQLite). It is never sent to external servers.

B. Workout Logs & Progress Metrics

  • Completed workout routines, exercise checkmarks, set counts, and repetitions.
  • Session timers, calculated calories burned, and daily consistency streak counters.
  • Where it is stored: Exclusively on your physical device.

C. Device Hardware & Permissions

  • Text-to-Speech (TTS) Voice Countdown: The App utilizes Android's built-in Text-to-Speech engine to call out workout timer counts and transition cues. We do not record or process audio, and no microphone permission is used.
  • Network Permission (INTERNET): Declared in AndroidManifest for basic web linking and support channels. We do not run third-party tracking networks or ad spyware.
  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: MRX WORKOUT does not request or access camera, contacts, microphone, photos, location/GPS, phone state, or SMS.

5. User Data Rights & Data Deletion

Because your data resides entirely on your device, you have full control over data deletion at all times:

  • In-App Reset: You can edit or change your profile data at any time under Settings > Edit Profile.
  • Device Storage Wipe: You can permanently wipe all workout logs and profile details anytime by going to:
    Android Settings > Apps > MRX WORKOUT > Storage > Clear Data.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all sandboxed files and saved records permanently.
  • Email Support Deletion: If you ever communicate with us via email and want your communication history deleted, email us at sonukumar4454596@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
