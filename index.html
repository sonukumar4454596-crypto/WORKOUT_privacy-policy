Summary: MRX WORKOUT is built with an offline-first privacy approach. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal health or workout data with third parties. All workout metrics, BMI calculations, and progress tracking are stored securely and directly on your local device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MRX WORKOUT ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Sonu Lal, collects, uses, and protects your information when you download, install, and use our Android mobile application.

We respect your right to privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed by the App in compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google's User Data policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable global privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect and Process

To provide personalized workout plans, rest timers, and exercise tracking, the App processes certain data categories. Because MRX WORKOUT operates primarily on-device, this data remains on your physical phone:

A. Fitness and Athlete Profile Parameters (Voluntarily Provided)

  • Physical Attributes: Gender, age range, height, current weight, and target goal (e.g., build muscle, lose fat, maintain stamina).
  • Workout Preferences: Target focus areas (Chest, Arms, Abs, Legs, Full Body), workout style (Hypertrophy, Strength, HIIT), experience level, and available gym/home equipment.
  • Storage: Stored strictly in local device storage (Android App Sandbox / LocalStorage). It is never sent to external commercial servers.

B. Workout Progress and Activity Logs

  • Completed workout sessions, sets, and rep checkmarks.
  • Rest timer durations and elapsed workout times.
  • Calculated calories burned estimates based on MET formulas.
  • Storage: Retained locally on your device for generating personal stats, streaks, and analytics graphs.

C. Device & System Permissions Used

  • Internet Permission (android.permission.INTERNET): Used only to fetch public CDN fitness visual assets (exercise animation previews) and handle standard web-view rendering. No background data synchronization occurs.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide physical haptic feedback when a workout rest timer ends.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): Uses on-device speech synthesis to announce upcoming exercises. No voice or audio recordings are captured or transmitted.

4. How We Use Your Data

The local data processed by the App is used exclusively for:

  • Generating customized workout routines matched to your fitness level and equipment.
  • Calculating Body Mass Index (BMI) and recommended weekly volume.
  • Providing audio cues and timers during exercise sets.
  • Displaying your progress history, completed workouts, and achievement badges.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party behavioral analytics, ad-tracking SDKs, or data brokers. Standard platform services include:

  • Google Play Services: Provided by Google LLC to facilitate app installation, updates, and OS compatibility. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Data Retention and Deletion (User Control)

Because all user data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and instantaneous control over it at all times:

  • In-App Reset: You can edit or overwrite your profile details at any time in the Settings > Edit Profile screen.
  • Complete Device Deletion: You can permanently wipe all saved workout history, metrics, and preferences by going to:
    Android Settings > Apps > MRX WORKOUT > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data .
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling the application automatically removes all sandbox files, preferences, and cached exercise records from your device.
  • Support Deletion Requests: If you have contacted us via email for support and wish to have your email correspondence deleted, email us at supportmrxworkout@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request", and we will purge all correspondence within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)

MRX WORKOUT is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate measures.

8. Security Safeguards

We value your trust in using MRX WORKOUT. Your data is isolated within Android's sandboxed application storage environment, preventing unauthorized access by other applications installed on your device.
